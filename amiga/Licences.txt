Laser Kombat: licences
======================

The game: Laser Kombat (1995) by David Dewey, who shared its code under the
GPL; the SDL 2 and C++17 version by Wouter Wijsman,
https://github.com/sharkwouter/laserkombat.

- The program, its levels, pictures and sounds (src/, include/, assets/)
  are under the GNU General Public License, version 3 (COPYING.txt).
- The fonts (assets/fonts: FreeSans, FreeSerif, FreeSerif Italic) are GNU
  FreeFont, under the GNU General Public License version 3 or later with
  the font exception; their authors are in assets/fonts/AUTHORS.
- The Amiga port's own files (amiga/, AMIGA_PORT.md, package/, tools/,
  tests/) are GPL 3, like the game; the GlowIcon art is The AmiCradle
  Team's own.

The game's data are upstream's own, unchanged.
